How goa Radar would run safely inside goa.

Explain it in 30 seconds

Radar reads public news, council and event listings every day. For each new opening, event or approval it says why it is a reason to call, finds the nearest goa billboards, scores how good the lead is, and drafts an email and a one-page proposal. Nothing is sent by the system: a rep checks the facts, edits, and sends it from Outlook. The score is a simple checklist (how certain, how close, who would buy, how soon, how good the source), not a prediction.

0. Two views, two audiences

Each opportunity has a 🔒 Rep brief (internal: score, talking points, objections, draft email, approval checklist) and a ✉ Client proposal (what the business owner sees: mockups, nearby boards, campaign builder, one call to action, no scores or internal notes). Flow: the rep reviews the brief, approves and sends the email from Outlook, and the client opens their personal proposal link.

1. Human approval before any send

The AI only drafts. Every email, proposal and follow-up sits in a review queue; a named rep must edit/approve it. No auto-send, no bulk send. Approvals are logged (who, when, what changed).

2. Citations required

A trigger can't enter the feed without a public source URL, and every pitch must reference it. Items with dead links or no source are dropped. The chat answers only from the feed and cites trigger ids.

3. Spam Act 2003 compliance

Every commercial email needs consent: express, or inferred (for example an existing business relationship, or an address conspicuously published where the offer relates to the recipient's business role and no "no spam" notice is shown). Each email identifies goa as the sender with contact details and includes a working unsubscribe, actioned within 5 business days. The rep records the consent basis before sending; opt-outs sync to a suppression list checked before any draft is created. Not legal advice: goa's own counsel would sign off.

4. No client data leaves goa systems

Public signals come in; client/CRM data stays in goa's Microsoft 365 / Salesforce tenancy. Production AI calls would use an enterprise endpoint with no training on inputs, logging in goa's tenancy, and only public trigger text + approved templates in prompts. Australian Privacy Principles apply.

5. Salesforce connection

Triggers create Leads/Tasks via the Salesforce API (connected app, OAuth, least-privilege integration user). Dedupe against existing Accounts first; approved emails log as Activities; stage changes feed the weekly digest.

6. Microsoft 365 connection

Drafts land in the rep's Outlook Drafts via Microsoft Graph (delegated permissions, Entra ID SSO). Reps send from Outlook themselves. Digest posts to a Teams channel. Power Automate can schedule the weekly run.

7. Source hygiene

Only public sources (council, government registers, news, venue listings), used within their terms; no logged-in or personal data. Site photos and site data in this demo come from goa's public website.

8. This demo

Unofficial; not built with any goa data except the public site locator. Pipeline digest uses clearly labelled sample data. No emails are sent from this site.
