Terrific Noodles (2nd site): outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Biang biang noodle specialist opened its second location at Westfield Chermside (level 2, near Event Cinemas). (Opened weekend of 3–4 Oct 2026). Source: The Weekend Edition – Pavement Whispers, 6 Oct 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new Terrific Noodles location at Westfield Chermside.

Audience

Brisbane North drivers and locals heading to Westfield Chermside and Event Cinemas.

Creative idea

Create bold noodle bowl visuals with the Terrific Noodles logo and a simple call to level 2 near Event Cinemas. Use the high impact format to stand out to hungry drivers on Gympie Road.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the weeks immediately following the 3-4 October 2026 opening weekend.
