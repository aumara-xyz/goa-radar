Samuel Bakehouse: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: New bakery at 132 Boundary Street, West End. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic for the new Samuel Bakehouse opening in West End.

Audience

Local residents, workers and visitors in the West End and Highgate Hill areas looking for fresh bakery options.

Creative idea

Create a mouth-watering visual series showing warm pastries and fresh bread with the simple tagline 'Now open in West End'. Use the nearby goa GRID sites to catch people walking or driving past the new venue every day.

Timing

Flight the campaign to begin in early 2026 and run through the first three months of confirmed trading.
