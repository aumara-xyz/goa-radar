Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from local businesses to the Melt Festival audience using nearby goa billboards.

Audience

Local businesses near New Farm and Brisbane Powerhouse seeking to engage the queer arts and culture festival attendees.

Creative idea

Create bold, colourful creative that celebrates queer expression and ties directly to the Melt Festival vibe. Use the high impact sites to welcome visitors and promote nearby offers as they travel to and from the Brisbane Powerhouse venue.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the full Melt Festival period from 21 October to 8 November 2026.
