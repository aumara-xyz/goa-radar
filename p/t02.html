Storiabella Gelateria: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: New premium gelateria at 222 Given Terrace, Paddington; grand opening 17 Oct 2026. (Grand opening Sat 17 Oct 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new Storiabella Gelateria grand opening in Paddington.

Audience

Dessert and gelato lovers in Brisbane Inner West looking for new local experiences.

Creative idea

Create mouth-watering visuals of vibrant gelato scoops against the Storiabella branding. Pair with a simple call to action directing people to visit the new Paddington store on opening weekend.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to and including the grand opening on 17 October 2026.
