Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new LUMA self-serve gelato location during its grand opening and four-week giveaway.

Audience

Dessert and gelato lovers living and working in Brisbane Inner North.

Creative idea

Create mouth-watering visuals of colourful self-serve gelato cups with the headline Try LUMA Newstead now open. Pair this with a simple call to action directing people to the giveaway at 60 Skyring Terrace.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the two weeks leading up to and including the grand opening on Thursday 15 October 2026.
