Objective

Build pre-launch awareness for Clutch Racing among local and visiting motorsport enthusiasts.

Motorsport fans, sim racing enthusiasts and local residents near Coomera and the Gold Coast.

Create high-impact racing-themed creative showing a simulator cockpit with bold 'Coming 2026' messaging. Use dynamic visuals of speed and competition to drive curiosity and foot traffic to the new venue.
