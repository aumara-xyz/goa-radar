10 Toes Brewing – The Loft: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: 10 Toes announced a third venue (The Loft) at its Buderim brewery and applied for an additional liquor licence. (Planned full operation by 10 Oct 2026). Source: The Crafty Pint, 23 Jun 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and trial of the new 10 Toes Brewing The Loft venue among Sunshine Coast beer drinkers.

Audience

Sunshine Coast locals and visitors who enjoy craft beer and frequent brewery bars.

Creative idea

Create a simple bold creative showing The Loft venue with an inviting call to visit the new Buderim spot. Use warm earthy tones and the 10 Toes logo to stand out on roadside billboards and connect with passing drivers.

Timing

Recommended flight from early September 2026 through to end of October 2026 to build anticipation and support the full operation by 10 Oct 2026.
