Pier 88: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: New venue on William Gunn Jetty, Manly; breakfast/lunch daily, Fri–Sat evenings. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and trial of the new Pier 88 venue among locals and visitors in Brisbane East.

Audience

Locals and visitors in Brisbane East interested in new cafe and restaurant experiences.

Creative idea

Create a mouth watering visual series showcasing Pier 88 breakfast dishes and waterfront views. Use the tagline New on the jetty to spark curiosity and encourage immediate visits.

Timing

Flight the campaign from early 2026 to coincide with the confirmed opening and first months of trading.
