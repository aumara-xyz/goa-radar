Queensland Ballet – The Nutcracker: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Christmas ballet season at QPAC. (9 – 19 Dec 2026). Source: Brisbane City Council – Top 10 shows

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from local businesses to the event audience attending The Nutcracker at QPAC.

Audience

Local businesses near South Brisbane seeking to engage with theatre-goers and Christmas event attendees.

Creative idea

Create festive digital creative that ties the business offering to the magic of The Nutcracker season. Use simple animated elements that stand out on THE goa GRID sites as audiences travel to and from QPAC.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the full Christmas ballet season from 9 to 19 December 2026.
