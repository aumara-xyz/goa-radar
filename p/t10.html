Eon: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Modern Asian restaurant at 451 Ann Street, Brisbane City. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and trial for the new Eon modern Asian restaurant opening in Brisbane CBD.

Audience

Brisbane CBD and inner city diners interested in modern Asian cuisine.

Creative idea

Create a striking visual series showing Eon signature dishes against the Brisbane skyline. Use bold typography to highlight the 2026 opening and invite passers by to discover modern Asian dining at 451 Ann Street.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the months leading up to and including the confirmed 2026 opening date.
