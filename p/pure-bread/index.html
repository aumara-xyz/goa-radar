Subject: Pure Bread Grand Opening in Graceville

Hi there,

To drive strong local awareness and foot traffic from day one, I recommend our nearby goa sites: the X-Track at Sherwood Railway Station just 1.1 km away or the 6x3m billboard at Belgrave Rd, Indooroopilly 2.5 km away. Both deliver high visibility to commuters and shoppers in Brisbane West.
