RLWC 2026: Australia v Fiji: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Rugby League World Cup men's match at Suncorp Stadium. (Sun 25 Oct 2026). Source: West End Central – Brisbane's Major Events 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from RLWC 2026 match attendees to local businesses near Suncorp Stadium.

Audience

Local businesses in Milton and West End seeking to engage rugby league fans attending the Australia v Fiji match.

Creative idea

Create bold rugby themed creative that highlights special event offers or extended trading hours. Use the iconic sites to build excitement and direct fans to nearby venues before and after the game.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the two weeks leading up to and including Sun 25 Oct 2026 to capture pre event buzz and match day crowds.
