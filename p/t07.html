Heuristic priority score from the factors above, not a prediction of sales outcomes.

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new Pure Bread bakery for its grand opening on 3 October 2026.

Audience

Local residents, commuters and food lovers in Brisbane West looking for fresh sourdough and bakery products.

Creative idea

Create mouth-watering visuals of steaming sourdough loaves and pastries with a clear call to visit on opening day. Pair this with a simple map showing the short distance from the billboards to the bakery at 293 Honour Avenue.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to the grand opening on Saturday 3 October 2026.
