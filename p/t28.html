Pan Pacific Masters Games: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: 40+ sports across Gold Coast venues; participant influx for 10 days. (6 – 15 Nov 2026). Source: Pan Pac Masters Games – About

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from event participants and spectators to local businesses near the venues.

Audience

Local businesses in Broadbeach and Surfers Paradise seeking to reach the influx of Masters Games participants and visitors.

Creative idea

Create bold, energetic creative that highlights local business offers with a fun Masters Games theme. Use high impact visuals of sports and the event dates to stand out to the active crowd travelling through the area.

Timing

Flight the campaign from early November through to 15 November 2026 to align with the full 10 day event period.
