Objective

Generate interest from a local business owner in using goa billboards to reach people attending the Pan Pacific Masters Games.

Audience

Local business owners near Broadbeach who may want to connect with event attendees.

Creative idea

The Pan Pacific Masters Games will bring a participant influx for 10 days across 40 plus sports at Gold Coast venues. Highlighting the listed goa sites by their numbers allows the business owner to consider options positioned along routes near the event area.

Timing

Contact the business owner ahead of the Pan Pacific Masters Games scheduled for 6 to 15 Nov 2026.
