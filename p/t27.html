Supanova Comic Con & Gaming: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Pop-culture expo at Brisbane Convention & Exhibition Centre. (Fri 6 – Sun 8 Nov 2026). Source: West End Central – Brisbane's Major Events 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from Supanova attendees to nearby local businesses using targeted out-of-home advertising.

Audience

Local businesses near the Brisbane Convention & Exhibition Centre in South Brisbane seeking to engage the pop-culture event crowd.

Creative idea

Create bold, colourful creative that taps into the fun and fandom spirit of Supanova. Feature simple calls to action inviting attendees to visit nearby stores for exclusive offers or themed experiences during the event weekend.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the three days of Supanova from Friday 6 to Sunday 8 November 2026.
