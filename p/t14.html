Bar Lou Lou: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: New wine bar and kitchen on Barry Parade, Fortitude Valley. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and trial for the new Bar Lou Lou wine bar opening in Fortitude Valley.

Audience

Fortitude Valley locals, inner north Brisbane residents and visitors looking for new wine bar experiences.

Creative idea

Create a series of elegant, warm-toned creative showing the Bar Lou Lou interior and signature wines to spark curiosity. Use a simple call to action inviting people to visit the new venue on Barry Parade when it opens in 2026.

Timing

Recommended flight to begin in early 2026 and run through the first three months of confirmed trading.
