Heuristic priority score from the factors above, not a prediction of sales outcomes.

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from local businesses to the Brisbane Good Food & Wine Show crowds using nearby goa GRID billboards.

Audience

Local businesses and food and drink operators in West End and Highgate Hill seeking to engage with event attendees.

Creative idea

Create mouth-watering creative that highlights special offers or extended trading hours during the show. Use bold food and wine imagery paired with clear directions to nearby venues to capture attention from passing event traffic.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the three days of the Brisbane Good Food & Wine Show from 23 to 25 October 2026.
