Bar Cooper's: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Neighbourhood wood-fired bistro at Shop 4, 321 Chatsworth Road, Coorparoo. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and trial for the new Bar Cooper's bistro opening in Coorparoo.

Audience

Local residents and passing drivers in Brisbane's Inner South who frequent neighbourhood bistros and restaurants.

Creative idea

Create a mouth-watering visual of the wood-fired dishes with a simple headline inviting the community to the new local. Use the proximity of the billboards to reinforce that Bar Cooper's is now open just around the corner.

Timing

Flight the campaign from early 2026 to coincide with the confirmed opening and initial trading period.
