150 Elizabeth Precinct (Wintergarden): outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Plans lodged to demolish the Wintergarden for a new CBD tower, hotel and laneway. (Reported 6 Oct 2026). Source: Brisbane Times, 6 Oct 2026

Objective

Raise awareness of the brand among Brisbane CBD workers, residents and visitors during the Wintergarden redevelopment announcement.

Audience

Businesses and developers active in or near the Brisbane CBD who want to reach local workers, residents and visitors.

Creative idea

Create bold, simple creative that acknowledges the change at Wintergarden while positioning the brand as part of Brisbane's next chapter. Use high contrast visuals and a clear call to action that ties the brand to progress and the future of the precinct.

Timing

Flight the campaign from mid October 2026 for a minimum of four weeks to capitalise on immediate interest following the 6 October announcement.
