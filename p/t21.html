Victory Tower, 127 Edward St: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Brisbane City Council approved a 51-storey, 284-room tower above the heritage Victory Hotel. (Approved 12 Oct 2026 (A006934633)). Source: The Australian Developer

Objective

Build awareness and drive enquiries for the new Victory Tower mixed-use development among Brisbane CBD professionals and visitors.

Audience

Developers, investors, hotel operators, retailers and businesses targeting Brisbane CBD foot traffic.

Creative idea

Create bold creative showcasing the 51-storey tower rising above the historic Victory Hotel. Use a simple before-and-after visual that highlights the exciting future of the precinct.

Timing

Flight the campaign from mid-October 2026 for eight weeks immediately following the 12 October 2026 approval.
