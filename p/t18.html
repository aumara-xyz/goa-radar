Objective

Drive awareness and visitation to local businesses among guests attending the Andaz Gold Coast grand opening event.

Audience

Luxury travellers, event attendees and local consumers visiting Broadbeach and Surfers Paradise for the Andaz opening.

Creative idea

Create elegant creative that mirrors the black-tie celebration with sophisticated imagery and an invitation-style headline. Position the billboard as a welcome to the new hotel while directing attention to nearby partner businesses offering dining, transport or experiences.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the four weeks leading up to the grand-opening event on 21 Nov 2026.
