Objective

Drive awareness and trial visits to the newly opened Coyotes Roadhouse among Sunshine Coast drivers.

Audience

Sunshine Coast locals and visitors travelling along key arterial roads near Maroochydore who enjoy country-western entertainment.

Creative idea

Feature bold cowboy imagery with the Coyotes Roadhouse logo and a simple call to action directing drivers to the new venue. Keep the creative clean and high-impact so it stands out to passing traffic on the highway.

Timing

Launch the campaign in late October 2026 to capitalise on the recent October opening.
