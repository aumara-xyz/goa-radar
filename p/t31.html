Heuristic priority score from the factors above, not a prediction of sales outcomes.

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from Lion King theatre audiences to nearby local businesses using goa GRID billboards.

Audience

Local businesses in South Brisbane and West End seeking to reach high volumes of event-driven theatre patrons and passing traffic.

Creative idea

Create bold Lion King inspired creative that taps into the excitement of the show while highlighting special offers or extended trading hours. Position the message on goa GRID sites so audiences see it on arrival and departure from QPAC.

Timing

Flight the campaign across the full 16 week Lion King season from 15 November 2026 to 7 March 2027.
