Objective

Generate interest from a local business owner in advertising to people attending the Lion King season at QPAC.

Audience

Local business owners near South Brisbane who want to connect with people attending events at QPAC.

Creative idea

Position goa billboards as a way to engage with attendees of the Lion King musical season. Highlight the proximity of the listed sites to the venue to support targeted local exposure.
