Urban Sports Bar: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Family-run sports bar opened at 13 The Esplanade, Maroochydore; hiring more staff. (Opened early Oct 2026). Source: Sunshine Coast News, 2 Oct 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and trial for the newly opened Urban Sports Bar among Sunshine Coast drivers.

Audience

Sports fans, locals and visitors travelling along the Sunshine Coast who are likely to visit a new sports bar.

Creative idea

Create bold, energetic creative showing the sports bar atmosphere with a clear call to visit 13 The Esplanade. Use bright team colours and simple messaging that highlights the new family-run venue to stand out on the roadside.

Timing

Flight the campaign from mid October 2026 for four weeks to capitalise on the early October opening.
