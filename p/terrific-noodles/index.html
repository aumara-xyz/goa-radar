Concept creative

Idea

Highlight the new opening at Westfield Chermside using the listed goa sites on Gympie Road. Present the sites as a way to build awareness among drivers in the surrounding area.

Objective

Secure a 15-minute call with the owner or manager to discuss billboard advertising options for the new restaurant location.

Audience

The owner or manager of Terrific Noodles responsible for local marketing decisions.
