Guns N' Roses with Airbourne: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Stadium show at Suncorp Stadium. (Sat 5 Dec 2026). Source: West End Central – Brisbane's Major Events 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from concert attendees to nearby local businesses using high-impact OOH near Suncorp Stadium.

Audience

Local businesses in Milton and West End seeking to reach Guns N Roses and Airbourne concert fans attending the Suncorp Stadium event.

Creative idea

Create bold rock-themed creative that taps into the energy of the Guns N Roses concert. Use the digital sites to rotate messages welcoming fans and highlighting nearby offers or venues.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to and including Sat 5 Dec 2026 to capture pre-event buzz and arrival day crowds.
