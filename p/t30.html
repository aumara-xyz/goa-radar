Objective

Drive brand awareness and foot traffic from RLWC 2026 finals attendees to local businesses near Suncorp Stadium.

Audience

Local businesses in Milton and West End seeking to engage sports fans attending the Rugby League World Cup finals.

Creative idea

Create bold countdown creative showing days until the double-header paired with a special offer for fans. Use high-impact imagery of rugby action and local venue details to drive immediate interest.

Flight the campaign across the two weeks leading up to Sun 15 Nov 2026 to capture pre-event excitement and arrival crowds.
