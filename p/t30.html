Objective

Secure a 15-minute call with the local business owner to discuss billboard advertising opportunities for the RLWC 2026 finals.

Audience

Local business owners near Milton and the Suncorp Stadium precinct who seek to connect with people attending the event.

Creative idea

Position the listed goa sites as targeted touchpoints for businesses wanting visibility among those heading to or from the World Cup finals double-header. Highlight the exact distances of the sites from the venue to demonstrate their relevance for capturing attention in the immediate area.

Timing

Approach local businesses in the weeks leading up to Sun 15 Nov 2026 to align advertising with the RLWC 2026 finals.
