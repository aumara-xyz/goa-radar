Surfers Paradise Coach Interchange: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: City of Gold Coast says the new interstate coach interchange on Cypress Avenue opens Nov 2026; Music Hall build starts Nov 2026. (Scheduled to open Nov 2026). Source: City of Gold Coast – project page

Objective

Drive awareness and capture attention from interstate visitors and locals at the new Surfers Paradise Coach Interchange from its opening.

Audience

Businesses seeking to reach high volumes of interstate coach travellers and Gold Coast visitors arriving at the new Cypress Avenue interchange.

Creative idea

Create bold, directional creative that welcomes interstate arrivals and guides them to nearby attractions or offers. Use the goa GRID format for high impact sequential messaging as people exit the interchange.

Timing

Flight the campaign to begin in November 2026 coinciding with the scheduled opening of the new coach interchange.
