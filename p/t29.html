Objective

Generate interest from a local business owner in using goa billboards to reach attendees of the Grand Diwali at Sea World.

Audience

Local business owners operating near Main Beach and Southport on the Gold Coast.

Creative idea

Position the listed goa sites as a way for the business to communicate directly with people heading to or from the cultural festival. Highlight the exact distances of the sites to the venue to show their relevance without exaggeration.

Timing

The email should be sent in advance of Sat 7 Nov 2026 to allow time for the business to plan advertising around the event date.
