Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from local businesses to the Grand Diwali at Sea World event crowd.

Audience

Local businesses near Main Beach and Southport that want to reach festival attendees on the Gold Coast.

Creative idea

Create vibrant Festival of Lights themed creative showing fireworks and cultural elements. Position the message to welcome Diwali visitors and highlight nearby business offerings.
