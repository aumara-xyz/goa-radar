Concept creative

Concept mockup – AI generated, not an actual goa placement. No real logos used; final creative from the client.

Idea

Highlight the three nearest goa sites by their site numbers and exact distances from the deli. Position the billboards as a way to drive initial customer awareness among passing traffic for the September or October opening.

Objective

Secure a 15-minute call with the owner or manager to discuss outdoor advertising options for the new deli.

Audience

The owner or manager of the new A Team Deli in Grange.
