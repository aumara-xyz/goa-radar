Idea

Create a mouth-watering creative showcasing the Japanese curry sandos and banana bread with a simple call to visit the new Grange location. Use bold colours and clean photography that matches the fresh deli offering to stand out on the goa GRID network.

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new A Team Deli in its opening period.

Audience

Local residents and workers in Brisbane's inner north suburbs looking for quality cafe and deli options.

Subject: Promote your new Grange deli

Hi there,

A new deli has opened on Days Road in Grange offering Japanese curry sandos and banana bread according to the Brisbane Times Good Food on 9 Oct 2026. This is a great opportunity to drive awareness and foot traffic while the business is fresh in the minds of locals.

I recommend using either of the two goa GRID sites at 434 Stafford Rd Stafford only 1.2 km away or the site at Enoggera Rd and Samford Rd Alderley 1.7 km away. Both deliver strong coverage of the inner north catchment.
