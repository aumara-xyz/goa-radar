South Bank Christmas & NYE fireworks: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Free nightly Christmas fireworks and the Lord Mayor's NYE fireworks at South Bank. (20–23 Dec & 31 Dec 2026). Source: Rambla – Brisbane Events Calendar 2026

Objective

Drive brand awareness and foot traffic from South Bank fireworks attendees to nearby local businesses.

Audience

Local businesses in South Brisbane and surrounding suburbs seeking to engage with seasonal event crowds.

Creative idea

Create festive countdown creative showing happy crowds enjoying fireworks with a clear call to visit your venue straight after. Use bright seasonal colours and simple directional messaging that ties directly to the South Bank location.

Timing

Run the campaign across 20-23 December and 31 December 2026 to align with the fireworks events.
