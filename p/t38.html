Objective

Generate interest from a local business owner in using goa billboards to reach people attending the South Bank fireworks.

Audience

Local business owners near South Brisbane who want to connect with people attending seasonal events.

Creative idea

Highlight the free nightly Christmas fireworks and Lord Mayor's NYE fireworks at South Bank as a seasonal event that draws local attendees. Position the listed goa sites as direct opportunities for a nearby business to promote to those attending without inventing any performance claims.

Timing

Align the proposal with the event dates of 20 to 23 Dec and 31 Dec 2026.
