Foo Fighters – Take Cover Tour: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Tour opener at Suncorp Stadium. (Thu 5 Nov 2026). Source: Rambla – Brisbane Events Calendar 2026

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from local businesses to the Foo Fighters concert crowd in Milton and West End.

Audience

Local businesses near Suncorp Stadium that want to reach concert attendees and passing traffic in Brisbane's Inner West.

Creative idea

Create bold, high energy creative nodding to the Foo Fighters tour with a clear call to action for nearby cafes, bars or retailers. Use the iconic format to own the roadside moment as fans head to and from the stadium.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to and including Thu 5 Nov 2026 to capture pre event hype and concert day crowds.
