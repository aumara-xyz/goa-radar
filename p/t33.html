Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from concert attendees to nearby local businesses using high-impact OOH advertising close to Suncorp Stadium.

Audience

Local businesses in Milton and West End seeking to reach fans attending the Robbie Williams concert at Suncorp Stadium.

Creative idea

Create bold, energetic creative that taps into the Britpop excitement and concert vibe. Use simple messaging that invites fans to drop in before or after the show for special offers or good times.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to and including Friday 20 November 2026 to capture pre-event hype and event day crowds.
