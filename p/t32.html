Kate Ceberano – Australian Made Tour: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Concert at The Events Centre, Caloundra. (Sun 15 Nov 2026, 7pm). Source: The Events Centre Caloundra

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from Sunshine Coast event attendees to local businesses near the concert route.

Audience

Local businesses near Steve Irwin Way and The Events Centre Caloundra seeking to reach concert-goers travelling to the Kate Ceberano show.

Creative idea

Create bold, high-visibility creative that ties directly to the Australian Made Tour and the 15 November date. Use simple directional messaging or venue references to capture attention from passing drivers heading to the event.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to Sunday 15 November 2026 to capture pre-event excitement and on-the-day travel.
