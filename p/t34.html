Iron Maiden: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Arena show at Brisbane Entertainment Centre. (Sun 22 Nov 2026). Source: Brisbane Entertainment Centre – Events

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic from Iron Maiden concert attendees to nearby local businesses.

Audience

Local businesses near Boondall and Deagon seeking to reach concert goers attending the Iron Maiden show.

Creative idea

Create bold, high energy creative that taps into the rock concert vibe with simple calls to action such as grab a bite before the show or cool down with a drink after. Use the proximity to the Brisbane Entertainment Centre to position the business as the perfect pre or post concert stop.

Timing

Flight the campaign in the two weeks leading up to and including Sun 22 Nov 2026 to capture fans planning and attending the Iron Maiden concert.
