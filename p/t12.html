Ilo Bakehouse: outdoor campaign proposal

Why now: Bakehouse at 199 Wynnum Esplanade, Wynnum. (Confirmed trading 2026). Source: Brisbane Food Guide – New Openings (checked 15 Oct 2026)

Objective

Drive awareness and foot traffic to the new Ilo Bakehouse opening in Wynnum.

Audience

Local residents and visitors in Brisbane East interested in fresh bakery products.

Creative idea

Create a mouth watering visual of warm baked goods with a simple headline welcoming the community to Ilo Bakehouse. Use the billboards to highlight the exact opening date and the convenient Wynnum Esplanade location.

Timing

Recommended flight to begin four weeks before the confirmed 2026 opening and run through the first month of trading.
