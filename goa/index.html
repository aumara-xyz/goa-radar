The walkthrough

From a news item to a proposal on the client's screen.

Eight steps. Each one is live in the demo; every screenshot below is from the real site.

01

Signals

Radar collects public, dated reasons to call: openings, events, approvals, school holidays. Every item links to its source.

Why it matters: Reps stop hunting; they start the day with a list of fresh, verifiable triggers.

See it live →
02

Score

Each trigger gets a transparent 0–100 priority score: certainty, distance to goa boards, who would buy, timing, source strength.

Why it matters: The best calls go first, and anyone can see why (no black box).

See it live →
03

Match to goa boards

Every trigger is matched to the nearest goa sites using goa's public site list and real coordinates.

Why it matters: The pitch is about a specific board, not 'billboards' in general.

See it live →
04

Rep brief

An internal page per opportunity: why now, talking points, objection handling, draft email and an approval checklist.

Why it matters: A rep can prepare a sharp call in minutes, and nothing goes out without a human sign-off.

See it live →
05

Client proposal

A personal page for the business owner: their concept on a nearby board, a map, a campaign builder and one call to action.

Why it matters: Prospects see themselves on a goa board before the first meeting.

See it live →
06

Weekly digest

A Friday summary of new triggers and pipeline movement (sample data in this demo).

Why it matters: Managers see activity and outcomes without chasing reports.

See it live →
07

Ask the feed

A chat that answers only from the feed, cites the trigger and refuses everything else.

Why it matters: Quick answers like 'what's opening near our Valley boards?' without searching.

See it live →
08

Guardrails

Human approval before any send, citations required, Spam Act 2003 consent and opt-out, client data stays in goa systems.

Why it matters: Safe to roll out to a sales team from day one.

Your first 90 days

Mapped to what success looks like in the role.

Based on the success criteria in goa's job ad: working tools, improved prototypes, an AI roadmap, a toolkit, training, measurable gains, governance and a project pipeline.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Listen and ship a first win

    • Shadow sales, trading, ops and leadership; list pain points and repetitive tasks
    • Harden goa Radar into a working tool the sales team uses daily
    • Agree governance: data, accuracy checks, responsible-AI rules
    Working toolsGovernance
  2. Weeks 3–6

    Connect it to how goa works

    • Salesforce: triggers become Leads/Tasks; approved emails log as Activities
    • Microsoft 365: drafts land in Outlook, digest posts to Teams, SSO via Entra ID
    • Improve existing prototypes into dependable assets with documentation
    Improved prototypesMeasurable gains
  3. Weeks 7–10

    Teach and multiply

    • Prompt and template toolkit: proposals, valid business reasons, research
    • Workshops and one-on-one coaching; people build their own assistants
    • Track time saved, usage and pipeline influenced
    ToolkitTraining
  4. Weeks 11–13

    Prove it and plan the next wave

    • Report measured gains against the baseline from week 1
    • Prioritised AI roadmap and project pipeline (reporting automation, proposal workflows, market-intelligence feeds)
    • Recommend what to scale, fix or stop
    AI roadmapProject pipeline

Next step after the demo: Salesforce + Microsoft 365 integration (weeks 3–6), so Radar lives where the team already works.
