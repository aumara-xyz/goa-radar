How it works

Two views per opportunity
  1. 1 · Radar spots a triggerPublic news, council or event listing, with its source link.
  2. 2 · 🔒 Rep reviews the briefInternal page: score, sites, talking points, draft email. The rep checks facts and consent.
  3. 3 · Rep approves and sendsFrom their own Outlook. Nothing is sent by the system.
  4. 4 · ✉ Client opens their proposalA personal page: mockups, map of nearby boards, campaign builder, one call to action.
